Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the System Siege RTS mobile application (the "Application") created by Tim Manning ("Service Provider") as an ad-supported service.

Information Collection and Use

The Application itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information directly. You can play the game without creating an account or submitting personal details.

Third-Party Services & AdMob Data Collection

The Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The Application itself does not directly collect or process user data. However, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and processes certain device data directly for advertising, analytics, performance diagnostics, and fraud prevention purposes. This data may include:

  • IP address (used to estimate general location)
  • Device and Account identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID, App Set ID)
  • User product interactions (app launches, ad impressions, video views, and taps)
  • Diagnostic information (app launch times, performance logs, and error/hang rates)

All data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). For more details on how Google collects and handles user data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms (AdMob).

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application does not use first-party cookies. However, third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs), such as Google AdMob, may utilize cookies, device identifiers, and local storage to deliver tailored or non-personalized advertisements in compliance with applicable local privacy laws.

Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under applicable privacy laws—including the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)—you have rights regarding personal data processed by third-party ad providers, including the right to access, delete, or limit the processing of your data.

You can manage, reset, or opt out of personalized advertising at any time directly through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through a third-party service integrated into this app, please contact the Service Provider so that appropriate deletion steps can be taken.
