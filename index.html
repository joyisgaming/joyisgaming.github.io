System Siege RTS

Simplified Real Time Strategy Game with an arcade twist.

Control planets to generate resources. Capture a planet by simply holding a ship in orbit, uncontested, until it changes to your team colour (always orange). Use the resources to increase fleet capacity and build more ships.

Destroy all the enemy space stations to win.
